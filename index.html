ACT I — THE ORDINARY WORLD

A Room of Hope, A World of Silence

🗣️

Two months ago, I was in a room filled with hope—a bootcamp of eager builders. Then one student revealed he had been scammed. We were lucky. We were there. We heard him. We were protected by that awareness.

🌍

But what about the thousands outside those four walls? The scammers were still out there. The Ministry's warning only reached those who already followed their pages. For the average citizen, the danger was invisible.

"I realized then that silence and isolation are a scammer's greatest allies.
And a government cannot fix a problem it cannot see."

ACT I — THE ORDINARY WORLD

The Invisible Crisis

🚫

Citizens Excluded

Many cannot read, write, or speak English fluently. Existing digital tools leave them voiceless.

📡

Problems Are Invisible

Issues scattered across radio call-ins, WhatsApp groups, and social media. No structured data for government.

⚖️

Prioritization is Broken

Without data, councils cannot tell which issues affect the most people or require urgent attention.

⏰

Small Issues Become Crises

Early detection is impossible. Problems escalate, costs multiply, public frustration grows.

THE STAKES

The Cost of Inaction

01

02

Public Frustration

03

Missed Opportunity
